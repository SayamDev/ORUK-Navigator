Caring for someone

A carer is a person who provides informal and/or unpaid support to a partner, family member, friend or neighbour and they could not manage without this help.


You could be supporting someone with finances, shopping, cooking meals, dressing, medication, providing personal and emotional support and/or helping them to access services.


This could be for many reasons such as mental and/or physical health issues, frailty, long term illness or substance misuse issues.
 

Tameside Carers Centre 

Here at Tameside Carers Centre, we can help you get the right support at the right time, to make life feel a little easier.



Our centre in Ashton is a space where people who look after someone to drop in for information, advice or simply just have a chat and a brew. 


We have a team of friendly wellbeing advisors who can provide tailored emotional support, advice on benefit entitlements such as Carer’s Allowance, signpost to local support services, social groups and much more. 


They can also help you to access Radar Keys, Message in a Bottle and Tameside Emergency Cards (TEC).

Carer’s Conversations

If you look after someone, you are entitled to this conversation under the Care Act 2014. This is a chance to talk about who you are, who you care for, what you do to help them and how this impacts you. 

Drop-in Support

Call in between 10am – 2pm on Monday, Tuesday, Thursday and Friday for information, advice or if you just want a chat and a brew! 
Appointments are not needed, just simply call in. 

Enjoy some time out at our coffee mornings on the last Tuesday of each month from 10am until 12noon. This is a relaxed session where you can talk to our Wellbeing Advisors, local services and meet other people who look after someone over a brew.

You can see pictures from our recent activities and events on our Facebook.
