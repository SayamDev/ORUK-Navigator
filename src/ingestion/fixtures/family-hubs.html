Family Hubs

Family Hubs provide an unprecedented level of support to children and parents/carers.

They act as a 'one stop shop' offering early support to families and young people to help overcome difficulties and build strong relationships.

As well as being open in buildings in each of Tameside’s four neighbourhood areas, with facilities also available at other community venues, they also include virtual offers and are for families with children of all ages, from 0 to 19 or up to 25 with special educational needs and disabilities (SEND), with a great Start for Life offer at their core.

By bringing existing family-help services together, they improve access and connections with support including help with infant feeding and perinatal mental health, as well as making it easier to access wider services such as smoking cessation and job advice.

Family Hubs are funded and supported by central Government. A Tameside Family Hubs Steering Group is providing action-focused leadership and oversight. Key partners in the programme include Tameside and Glossop Integrated Care NHS Foundation Trust, Tameside and Glossop Pennine Care, Action Together, schools, Active Tameside, libraries, Greater Manchester Police and HomeStart.

Parent & Carer Panels

Parent and Carer Panels are a great opportunity to influence how services supporting families during pregnancy, birth and early parenthood are delivered in Tameside. A Parent and Carer Panel is made up of parents and carers of children aged 0-2 years, as well as local service leads to ensure what is being offered by the Hub works for local families. The panels will also be asked to consider new ideas and asked for views on improvements. Our ambition is to have a panel for each Family Hub, reflecting local needs and aspirations. Panels will meet every two months, online or in person, with no commitment to come to all meetings. To find out more about joining a panel, please email us at familyhubpanels@tameside.gov.uk
